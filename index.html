Governance & security

Least privilege, end to end.

Isolated by design

Dedicated workspace, private networking, no public IPs. Other customers simply don't exist in your world.

Least-privilege access

Your team reads exactly the data products your license includes, nothing more. Personal data, where present, is processed in line with GDPR.

Production sealed off

No customer holds any permission on production data; curated views are the only path.

Governed as code

Every workspace, grant and permission is versioned, peer-reviewed and auditable.

In your workspace, you can

  • Query data products in your plan
  • Build dashboards, notebooks and reports
  • Use AI to analyze your data in plain language
  • Put machine learning models into production

By design, you can't

  • Access raw production data
  • See other customers, or be seen by them
  • Change platform-level permissions
  • Export data without the proper licensing and authorization
